# Long-term strategy PDFs to download by hand (23 files, 20 countries)

The UN climate portal (unfccc.int) is behind bot protection, so it can't be fetched by script. Please open each link in your own browser, save the PDF, and **rename it exactly as shown** into `final_project/ndc/data/raw/lts_pdfs/`.

Tips: the Czech 2025 update and the Ukrainian 2026 strategy are dated after our parliament data ends (2022); the earlier versions (CZ_2, UA_2) are the ones most relevant. Belgium's strategy is in French.
