Label climate passages

1 = climate: the passage is about climate change, greenhouse-gas emissions or climate policy.
0 = not: it is not (e.g. "business climate", "political climate", "carbon" in another sense).
? = unsure: you cannot tell from the passage (counted as 0).
Keys: 1, 0, ? (or u) to label and move on · left/right arrows to move without labelling. Your answers are saved in this browser as you go, so you can close the page and come back. When you are done, click Download CSV and move the file into final_project/ndc/data/processed/ (name it cutoff_labelling_sheet_labelled.csv).
