The niches as a map of communities

Test figure, not yet in the paper. The 2,618 commenters of r/StopEatingSeedOils fall into 12 communities of people who talk about similar topics (Louvain on the backbone). Here each community is one circle, so you see how the communities relate instead of a haze of dots.

Figure A. How the communities connect

Circle area is the number of people who have appeared so far: press play and each circle grows as its people first comment. A line means the two communities share more backbone links than their sizes would suggest; thicker is closer.

What to be careful about

Link strength is the number of backbone links between two communities divided by the number expected if people linked at random in proportion to how many links each community has (1 = as many as by chance). All pairs are below 1 because people link mostly inside their own community ( of links). The layout puts closer communities nearer, but distances are only a guide. Community names are ours, from each community's most over-represented topics. Disapproving tone means a GoEmotions score of 0.5 or more on anger, annoyance, disapproval or disgust, checked by hand on 150 comments: about 4 in 5 flagged comments are critical or hostile, about 1 in 4 hostile toward people. A community shows who talks about what, not that these people talk to each other.
