Did new user niches appear in 2024?

Test figures, not yet in the paper. A niche is a group of commenters who share a narrow topic focus (the communities of the backbone figure). We count how many people are active in each niche per month, before and after 1 January 2024, and ask whether the same niches can be found when the method is run on each period alone.

What to be careful about

The niches were found from everyone's whole history, and a newcomer's activity is all from 2024 by definition, so niches with many newcomers will show growth. Figure A and B are therefore not independent of the newcomer finding. Before 2024 there are far fewer people to form communities, so "no match" can mean there were too few people at that time, not that nobody cared. The second-pass matching compares small communities (7 to 32 people before 2024) with larger ones. Similarity scores between 0.4 and 0.6 are not clear either way. Niche names are ours.
