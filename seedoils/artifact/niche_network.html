What to be careful about

Disapproving tone means a GoEmotions score of 0.5 or more on anger, annoyance, disapproval or disgust, checked by hand on 150 comments: about 4 in 5 flagged comments are critical or hostile, about 1 in 4 hostile toward people, and mostly disapproval. Downvoted means a score of 0 or lower. Each person has only 10 to 30 comments in the topic model, so a person's own share is shrunk toward the average (as if they had 15 extra average comments) and a single dot says little; read the colour of a whole area. People who first commented before 2022 appear at the start of the slider. The 788 heavy commenters with no link are not drawn. The niches were found from everyone's whole history.
