What to be careful about

Counts are the number of comments that contain the term, per 10,000 counted terms in each group. Both groups are compared in the same period (2024 on), so differences are about who writes, not about time. The old guard is comments by 1,599 people and newcomers by 14,013, so rare terms are noisier for the old guard. Only terms used by at least 15 different people are included, so one person's habit cannot make a term. Newcomer means a first comment from 2024 on, so a newcomer's early comments and later comments are all in the newcomer group. The scores say what differs, not why: newcomers may be a different crowd, or the same people at an earlier stage of learning the vocabulary.
