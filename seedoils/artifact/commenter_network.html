Communities are named by me from their most over-represented topics. Hover a row to highlight it on the map.

How the backbone was made, and what to be careful about

The communities are built from what people talk about, so each one is a topic niche by construction. They show who occupies each niche, not that these people interact or know each other. Results depend on the filter: a stricter setting leaves more people unlinked and a looser one makes the communities less stable. Only comments of 15 words or more are used, and "long-time" means a first comment in the subreddit before 2024.
