\begin{frame}{When a seed-oil community tripled, what did it talk about?}
\begin{columns}[T]
\begin{column}{0.55\textwidth}
\textbf{Question.} r/StopEatingSeedOils grew \key{3$\times$} from 2023 to 2024. Did its conversation change: which topics grew or shrank, and when? (Not: what caused it.)\\[5pt]
\textbf{Data.} \key{149\,815} comments and posts, 19\,356 accounts, 2022--2026.\\[5pt]
\textbf{Course tools: text and networks}
\begin{itemize}\setlength{\itemsep}{1pt}
\item \textbf{Topic model} (BERTopic): 123 topics, followed \key{by quarter}
\item \textbf{Co-writing network}: topics linked when the same people write in both
\item \textbf{Emotions}: a model trained on Reddit comments
\end{itemize}
\end{column}
\begin{column}{0.41\textwidth}
\colorbox{accent!8}{\parbox{0.94\linewidth}{\small\textbf{Can we trust the topics?}\\[3pt]
\key{71\%} of documents get a topic; 29\% stay untagged.\\[3pt]
We read \key{150} comments: \key{80\%} fit their topic, the same for documents the clustering placed itself and for those rescued with confidence $\geq$ 0.02.}}
\end{column}
\end{columns}
\end{frame}

\item \textbf{Negative emotion} was flat in 2022--23, then \textbf{stepped up in 2024} (6.3\% $\to$ 8.2\% of documents by mid-year) and crept up to $\sim$9.5\%; mostly \textbf{within} topics, not tied to the political spike
