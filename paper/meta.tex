\newcommand{\papertitle}{From fatty acids to politics: how a seed-oil community on Reddit changed topic in 2024 (working title)}
\newcommand{\paperkeywords}{seed oils $|$ Reddit $|$ topic models $|$ BERTopic}
\newcommand{\abstracttext}{The Reddit community built around claims that seed oils are harmful grew about threefold in 2024. Using 149{,}815 comments and posts from r/StopEatingSeedOils, we ask what the community talked about before and after that surge. A topic model (BERTopic) found 123 topics, which we followed quarter by quarter from 2022. Most large topics kept their share of the conversation while activity grew about tenfold at its peak; the clearest exception, the chemistry of omega-6 fatty acids, lost share and partly recovered. A few small topics about politics, vaccines and the food industry spiked in 2024 and faded within about two quarters, and a network of who wrote in which topic shows the political topics forming their own bundle, taken up by long-time members and newcomers alike. We check the topic assignment by reading samples of comments and against a second topic method, and we do not identify what caused the surge.}
% PNAS: 50 to 120 words, for an educated scientist outside the field (the course asks for 2-3 sentences, so aim for the short end).
\newcommand{\significancetext}{Real-world events can quickly reshape what an online community talks about. In one large Reddit community about seed oils, most of the conversation kept its place when the community grew in 2024, while a short burst of political, vaccine and food-industry topics came on top and then faded. We show how to follow such shifts in trending topics without labelled data.}
