\newcommand{\papertitle}{From fatty acids to politics: how a seed-oil community on Reddit changed topic in 2024 (working title)}
\newcommand{\paperkeywords}{seed oils $|$ Reddit $|$ topic models $|$ BERTopic}
\newcommand{\abstracttext}{The Reddit community built around claims that seed oils are harmful grew about threefold in 2024. Using 149{,}815 comments and posts from r/StopEatingSeedOils, we ask what the community talked about before and after that surge. A topic model (BERTopic) found 123 topics, which we grouped by hand into 14 themes. The conversation moved quickly from the chemistry of fatty acids towards politics, vaccines and a general distrust of industry and medicine. The political wave peaked in late 2024 and had largely faded by 2026, while distrust stayed above its earlier level. We check the result against a second topic method and by reading samples of comments. The themes are our own design choices, and we do not identify what caused the surge.}

\newcommand{\significancetext}{Real-world events can quickly reshape what an online community talks about. In one large Reddit community about seed oils, conversation shifted in 2024 from the chemistry of fatty acids to politics, vaccines and a general distrust of industry, and then partly faded back after the end of the year. We show how to follow such shifts in trending topics without labelled data.}
